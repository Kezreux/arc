
# General terminology used for this project

## ARC
Awesome Radio Controlled - acronym for the car itself.

## Remote controller
The handheld radio controller one uses to operate the car from a distance.

## Control Module
The totality of control circuits operating the ARC - covering everything from charging to the remote controller.

## Direct Current (DC)
Current flowing from one pole to the other, usually from positive to negative. This gives a clean linear current and voltage.

## Alternating Current (AC)
The current flows back and forth between two phases, at either 50Hz or 60Hz, meaning times per second. This gives a sinusoidal waveform of the voltage and current.
